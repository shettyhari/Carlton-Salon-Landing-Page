FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the starting capital contribution?

The indicative capital contribution is ~₹1.0 Cr for a Signature Salon and ~₹1.5 Cr for a Grande Salon. Final budgets vary by city, site and format.

What formats are available?

Signature Salon (1,000–1,500 sq ft) and Grande Salon (1,800–2,500 sq ft). You can partner for a single salon, or speak with our team about a larger growth partnership.

What is the Monthly Minimum Payout Protection?

Where contractually applicable, the Business Partner receives a Monthly Minimum Payout Protection of ₹2L per month on a ~₹1.0 Cr Signature Salon and ₹3L per month on a ~₹1.5 Cr Grande Salon. Each month, the payout is this amount or 20% of total centre revenue, whichever is higher. The exact payout terms are set out in the franchise agreement.

Who runs the salon?

Carlton is partner-funded and company-operated. You bring the capital, local property support and city insights. Carlton delivers the brand, design, hiring, training, SOPs, procurement, marketing, CRM and day-to-day operations.

What does the capital contribution cover?

Interiors and fit-out (35–40%), equipment and furniture (20–25%), launch marketing (10–12%), opening inventory (8–10%), working capital (10–12%) and brand systems and setup (5–7%).
